How your balance is calculated — exactly like your Google Sheet

1. Status: IF(TODAY() - Hiring Date > 90, "Passed", "Probation") — Probation staff accrue but need admin approval.
2. Accrual: ROUNDDOWN( (Days since 1 Jan 2026 or Hiring Date if later ÷ 30) × 1.67 ÷ 0.5 ) × 0.5 — 20 days/year = 1.67/month, rounded down to nearest 0.5 day.
3. Eligible: Accrued + 2025 Remaining (carry-over) − Taken Annual — this is the hidden column J in “Overall Summary”.
4. Taken Annual (COUNTIFS from Leaves Log): Full Day = 1.0, Half Day = 0.5, 1 Hour = 0.125, 2 Hours = 0.25, 3 Hours = 0.375 (8-hour day). Sick / Unpaid / Eid / Other are counted separately and do NOT reduce Annual.
Example — Salam Sami: Hired 15-Jun-2026, 2025 Remaining = 0, Accrued by Nov = 2.1, Taken Annual = 5.9 → Remaining = 2.1 − 5.9 = −3.8 (overused) — system will warn admin. Haveen: Eligible 7.8, Taken 15.8 — see full table in Balances.
Hidden columns: Column A = Department group (Ops / CRM / Fleet), Column H = Status formula, Column I = 2025 Remaining (manual carry-over), Column J = Eligible formula above, Columns K–O = Taken by type via COUNTIFS. Nothing else is hidden — all balances come from Leaves Log + Hiring Date.
